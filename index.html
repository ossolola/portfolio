Flagship · n8n · SaaS
Speech Lab Pro
An AI-powered public speaking performance analyser built as a white-label SaaS platform. Accepts a 2 to 5 minute audio or video recording, transcribes it using Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, then runs it through 7 parallel AI analysis modules scoring the speaker across Filler Words, Speech Pace, Tone Variation, Energy Level, Clarity, Confidence, and Emotional Modulation. Each dimension gets a formula-backed score out of 100, feeding into a weighted composite score and one of 5 performance tiers from Foundational to Elite.

A 12-drill recommendation engine selects training exercises based on the speaker's specific weak spots, assembles a 7-day personalised training plan, and GPT-4o writes a coaching narrative addressed to the speaker by name. The full report is generated as a branded PDF and delivered to their inbox within 3 minutes.
Audio Upload → Gemini 2.5 Flash → 7 AI Modules → GPT-4o Narrative → PDF Report → Gmail Delivery

UiPath · In Progress
AI-Powered Customer Service Email Automation
Building an end-to-end RPA workflow in UiPath that automates triage of customer service emails for a banking environment, reducing manual handling of incoming enquiries. The automation ingests emails via IMAP, filters out noise such as auto-replies and no-reply senders, and uses the OpenAI API (GPT-4o-mini) to classify each email into business categories including Complaint, Pricing, Onboarding, Refund, Technical Support, and General Enquiry.

A grouping and response system batches categorised emails and will generate AI-drafted, empathetic response templates per category to speed up agent reply turnaround. Automated agent assignment and notification logic routes each email category to the responsible team via email and Microsoft Teams alerts. The build also includes UiPath Cloud Orchestrator infrastructure: unattended robot runtimes, Orchestrator Assets for secure credential storage, queue-based transaction tracking, and scheduled triggers for autonomous execution, alongside REST API integration with OpenAI for JSON parsing, HTTP request configuration, and authentication across mail and AI services.
